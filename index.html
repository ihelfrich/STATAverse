The command is only
part of the work.

A table of coefficients can be correct and still be misread. The workspace pairs each exercise with an interpretation check, and makes the sample, units and assumptions visible.

Work through missing values →
A common Stata surprise 
. count if hours > 5
12

. count if hours > 5 & !missing(hours)
11

Numeric missing values compare greater than ordinary numbers in Stata. One extra observation enters the first calculation. The second command makes the intended sample explicit.
